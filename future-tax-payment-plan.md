
# how to pay federal taxes

pay 4450/quarter to the IRS

🏛️ Part 1: How to Pay the IRS (Federal)

You will make your federal payments through the official IRS portal.

1. Go to the **[IRS Payments Gateway](https://www.irs.gov/payments)**.
2. Click the blue button that says **"Pay Now with Direct Pay"** (this transfers funds directly from your bank account for free).
3. On the next screen, click **"Make a Payment."**
4. You will see three dropdown menus. Select these exact options:
    - **Reason for Payment:** Estimated Tax
    - **Apply Payment To:** 1040ES (for 1040 federal estimated tax)
    - **Tax Year for Payment:** 2026 (or the current tax year you are filing for)
5. **Verify Your Identity:** The system will ask you to enter information from a prior year's tax return (like your filing status and address) to confirm who you are.
6. **Enter Payment details:** Input your bank routing/account number and your payment amount (**~$4,450**).
7. **Save the Receipt:** Print or save the confirmation page as a PDF for your accountant.

# how to pay state taxes

pay 1150/quarter CA franchise tax board

🌴 Part 2: How to Pay the California FTB (State)

California uses a different system called Web Pay.

1. Go to the **[California FTB Web Pay Portal](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/pay/index.html)**.
2. Under the "Bank Account" option, click **"Pay online with Web Pay."**
3. Select **"Personal"** (even though it is business income, as a sole proprietor, it passes through to your personal state taxes).
4. Enter your SSN, Name, and Date of Birth to log in.
5. On the payment type screen, select:
    - **Payment Type:** Estimated Tax (Form 540-ES)
    - **Tax Year:** 2026 (or current tax year)
6. Enter your bank information and payment amount (**~$1,150**).
7. **Save the Receipt:** Keep this confirmation text alongside your federal receipt.

# how to catch up on missed deadlines

🚀 Step 1: Make a "Catch-Up" Payment Immediately

Do not wait for the next formal deadline or until you file your taxes next Spring. Go online to the IRS and California FTB portals tonight or tomorrow and pay what you can.

- **If you have the cash saved:** Pay the total amount that should have been submitted for the missed quarters so far.
- **If you don't have it all right now:** Pay whatever amount you can realistically afford. Any payment amount freezes the daily penalty accumulation on that portion of the tax bill.

🛡️ Step 2: Leverage the "Safe Harbor" Rule

You might actually avoid a penalty completely if you meet the **Safe Harbor** requirements. The IRS and California will not penalize you for missed quarterly payments if your W-2 withholdings and any payments you make now equal:

1. **90%** of what you owe for the current tax year, OR
2. **100%** of what you owed on your previous year's tax return (110% if your income was over $150,000).

Because your W-2 job starts in August, it will automatically begin withholding taxes. If your total tax bill last year was very small, your W-2 job's withholdings combined with a catch-up payment now might protect you entirely under the 100% prior-year rule.

📝 Step 3: Ask for Penalty Relief (First-Time Abate)

If this is your first year running a business and you do end up with an IRS penalty next spring, you can call them and verbally request a **First-Time Penalty Abatement**. The IRS will usually wipe out failure-to-pay penalties for taxpayers who have a clean filing history from the previous three years.

📅 Step 4: Use the Annualized Income Method

When you file your taxes next year, your tax preparer (or software like TurboTax) can use **IRS Form 2210 (Annualized Income Installment Method)**. This form tells the IRS, _"Hey, my tutoring business made most of its money later in the year, and my W-2 job didn't even start until August."_ This recalculates your penalties so you aren't charged for missing deadlines early in the year when you hadn't actually earned the money yet.
