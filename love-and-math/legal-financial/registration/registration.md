# Registering in San Francisco
For assistance with business registration, filing a Fictitious Business Name (FBN), and licenses/permits with the City of San Francisco, please reach out to the San Francisco Office of Small Business (OSB). You can schedule a virtual meeting with an OSB business services manager by calling (415) 554-6134 OR an in-person meeting via this link: [https://kiosk.na5.qless.com/kiosk/app/home/513](https://kiosk.na5.qless.com/kiosk/app/home/513 "https://kiosk.na5.qless.com/kiosk/app/home/513") .
