

I've been developing this workshop over the past few months and am super excited to announce it. I hope it will be the first of many aimed at satisfying and promoting mathematical curiosity, while having fun in a small group environment with plenty of hands-on activities and mind-bendy exercises.

Infinity is a great place to start, because we all have ideas about what it means. But what is infinity? Can we touch it, count it, see it, arrive at it? What kinds of paradoxes arise or resolve when we extend our intuition for number and space to include infinity? 

Join me this August to explore these questions and more.
