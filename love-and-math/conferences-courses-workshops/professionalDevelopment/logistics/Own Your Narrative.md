# What Makes a Story Newsworthy?
PR compels attention from journalists, media, and the public. To earn coverage, your story must meet the media's standard of newsworthiness.

Timely
	Connected to what's happening right now in culture, policy, or business.
Novel
	A fresh angle, an expansion, an investment—something new worth talking about.
Human
	Stories that pull heartstrings and reflect real community impact resonate most.
Relevant
	Speaks directly to the audience the outlet serves. Know your outlet, know their reader.

Use AI tools to quantify media value. Measure impressions, monitor coverage, and demonstrate ROI from every story placed.
