# negotiating commercial lease agreements
**LOI vs Lease**
	**LOI** letter of intent, non-binding, documents important terms of lease
	**Lease** legally binding, expansive, contains all terms
	Both are negotiable
**What is negotiated?**
	leaseholder details
	term
	rent amount
	lease structure
	rent increases
	condition of premises
	use, operating hours
	lease and rent commencement dates
	option terms
	sublease terms
	personal guaranty
**What is negotiated in an LOI?**
	are utilities separately metered?
	tenant improvements and maintenance
	insurance policies to hold
	property owner improvements/maintenance
	ADA improvements
**Understanding the condition of the premises**
	confirm zoning and use
	obtain build out or improvement estimates
	negotiate and write down delivery condition, if any
**Do your research**
	visually inspect the space and ask questions
	confirm info advertised or verbally shared
	obtain several estimates from industry professionals
	consult with a permit specialist at the OSB counter
	confirm if the utilities are separately metered
**Lease Structures**
	Gross
		monthly base rent to property owner
		tenant pays utilities
	Percentage rent
		typical for retail and food/beverage tenants
		tenant pays a lowered minimum base rent and a percentage of gross sales
	Triple Net Lease (NNN)
		Monthly base rent to owner
		additional monthly pass through of charges like
			building property taxes
			building property insurance
			common area maintenance fees
		Pass through charges are non-negotiable
		confirm in the lease that you have the right to audit the charges annually

	Who should review your lease?
		yourself

		Sublease/assignment clause
		dispute resolution clase
		first right of refusal or lease honored upon...
