Alexis, question for you: What is a best practice for you?
Most helpful practice is making it comfortable for customers to ask for what they need. Access needs are human needs, and they change. Good service leaves room for those changes.

Debbi, question for you: What's the best starting place for a business with modest resources?
Understand that the accessible bathroom doesn't mean the extra space gets used for storage. One big challenge is getting to a table when a restaurant is crowded--may involve asking others to move. Most positive experiences has been when the person in charge has been willing to help me make the request of the other patrons. What kind of atmosphere does the space have--are people treated generously in general? If so, then people are more amenable to being flexible and accommodating to others.
Anything to add, Alicia?
