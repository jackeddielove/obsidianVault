	Restoring participation in our math classes
		Chang the perception of who's smart in math
		Create class structures that are equitable
		Modify lessons to be more group-worthy
		Create safe spaces instead of competitive ones
		Change the expectations of what it means to do math
	Traditional Expectations
		Students come in thinking we have these expectations of them
		- always raise their hand
		- work/calculate fastest
		- first one done
		- always right
	Change Expectations!
		How do we learn together as a community, build our communal knowledge?
		- tried again after mistake
		- explained to someone else
		- built off strategies of others
		- asked questions until they understood
		- shared the ideas of someone else
