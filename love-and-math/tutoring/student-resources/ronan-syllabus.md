### In-Class Assessments and Feedback

Assessments will be given in the form of quizzes and tests.

**Quizzes will be assessed via rubrics** and function as incentives for students to make the most of the practice time in order to comfortably meet our Learning Goals and also as opportunities to demonstrate mastery of the subject material. After students get quizzes back, they will be reviewed as a class in order to bolster their understanding and build a foundation for excellence on Tests. Quizzes have an **open-notebook policy**.

**Tests will be assessed via rubrics** and are the heaviest contributors to a student’s final assessment. Tests have an **open-notebook policy.** Tests which are assessed Approaching and below **will be able to be retaken** during office hours/Extra Help/etc. **as many times as desired with one condition**: students must use their **own time** to correct and redo the problems they wish to retry, and then come to me with the corrections and explain their thought processes, where they went wrong, etc.

**At least two major projects** will take place over the course of the year, and will serve as a student’s Landmark Experiences in mathematics, as well as contributing to a student’s overall assessment.

### In-Class Practice and Homework

Every day, students will be practicing the subject material in class in one way or another. The subject material can be challenging, so practice will require students to engage in the CDS Core Skill of **Research and Inquiry** and the Core Dispositions of **Curiosity and Open-Mindedness** and **Resilience and Persistence**. Homework happens naturally when a student does not have enough time in class to finish practice problems in class. Students can use whatever tools or resources they would like to finish up work at home – **it is up to them to use only those tools which will also benefit them on quizzes and tests**. Time is reserved at the beginning of class for students to ask questions about the prior lesson’s practice problems & homework. Students can and should use the Extra Help time (and/or make appointments with me) for extra individual instruction and practice. This all corresponds with the CDS Core Skills of **Self-Direction** and **Critical and Creative Thinking.**

With few exceptions, practice (including homework) can only directly boost a student’s assessment by improving their performance on In-Class Assessments (which includes homework). However, practice can negatively affect a student’s assessment if there is a sense that a student is not putting in reasonable effort, **especially by either not doing it or by distracting other students during practice time**. Mathematics in the real world is a collaborative effort and effective collaboration and as such, effective collaboration can be a contributor to a student’s assessment, either **positively** **by helping others** or **negatively by distracting other students**. This corresponds to the CDS Core Skills of **Collaboration** and **Communication** and Core Disposition of **Emotional/Social Intelligence.**

### Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence and generative AI assistant tools are not used in class. If students choose to use AI tools at home during practice time, it is up to them to use them intelligently, lest their in-class assessment performance suffers. I am open to any and all questions on the intelligent use of AI for middle school mathematics.
