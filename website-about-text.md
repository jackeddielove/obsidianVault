# Reason for being

I created Love and Math to bring together my loves for math, teaching, and making with our shared need to connect. It is a creative space where we work together and grow. Through one-on-one tutoring, group sessions, and creative workshops, Love and Math exists to bring us together and transform how we see ourselves and what we are capable of. Along the way, we just might change how we see mathematics and our relationship to it. Math is more than what we see in textbooks and homework assignments, more than a toolbox of equations for calculating interest or engineering bridges. Math is a practice of applying our creativity, intuition, and experience to solve problems and develop new understanding.
# About me

I wasn't always a "math person". In high school I wanted to be an artist or a poet or an architect. I wanted to create beautiful things. Math was all well and good, and I could follow directions well enough to get the answer in the back of the book, but it didn't seem like much more than that. It wasn't until my 20s that I started developing a deeper appreciation for the subject. This was due in part to the visual aspects of mathematics that I got to explore in multivariable calculus, and in part to recreational reading about mysterious mathematical objects like fractals and foundational ideas like Godel's Incompleteness Theorem that seemed deeply philosophical, in a way addressing the nature of mathematical truth itself. As I continued my studies at university, my ideas of what mathematics is were constantly challenged and changed and expanded. Math became deeper, more human, more beautiful. My tendencies as a would-be artist or architect to express my aesthetic sensibilities found a new outlet in mathematics. Here was an endless realm of discovery and an opportunity to make beautiful things indeed. The more I learned, the more I wondered why no one ever told me this is what math is!
