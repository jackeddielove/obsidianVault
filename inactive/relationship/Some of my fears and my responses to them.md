Fear #1: The fear that I've already done too much damage. That now that I'm finally ready, it's too late. It seems like any rational observer would tell you to leave, and I'm sure there is a part of you, maybe most of you, maybe all of you, that is saying that, too. It hurts me to think of not being able to be with you, but it hurts more to think of the pain I've caused you that has made it so. Of course if you ultimately realize that it is best for you to leave, best for your soul, best for your whole being, to leave, then of course that is what I want for you.

Response to fear #1: This is out of my control, really. So there is no reason to dwell on it. All I can do is show up and be honest, which is what I want to do whether or not you stay in/return to this relationship.

Fear #2: Fear that we stay together and I go to the opposite extreme of pushing you away. I become clingy, I make you my higher power (as they caution against in AA). The power of some of the feelings I've had toward you these past few days has felt almost pathological and I'm afraid it may be just that, some kind of disease of the mind and heart. Codependency, I guess.

Response to fear #2: I'm not too worried about this. But it is something I have to keep my eye on. I've noticed it pop up. I've done it before where I go overboard. I think I thought that's what love was, going overboard. I do want to go overboard *with you*, though. Like when you said the other day that part of you wants to fall into me. I'd love to do that with you. Really what this makes me think of is that there is always more to learn about love and partnership and myself and you, and I want to do that with you. Just keep learning. And if I can't do it with you I want to keep doing it anyway. I want to keep growing.

Fear #3: Fear that we stay together and I give you my whole heart and it turns out it's not something you want. That when I'm finally open and vulnerable, you'll see some part of me for the first time and you won't like it.

Response to fear #3: I don't know, I think you know me pretty well by now. And you're kind and loving and the opposite of petty and I just think that your commitment to love is greater than some little flaw you might find in me. After all, you've already seen some pretty major ones. And if there really is some part of me that is like fundamental to who I am, and it truly doesn't work for you are fundamentally, then I guess we don't belong together. I would hope that the kind of relationship we can build from here would be able to address that with love and kindness and wisdom.

Fear #4: One more fear that I have is that this feeling I've had over the past week, of feeling born anew with an open heart toward love and partnership, will be fleeting. I'm afraid the power of the moment I had in the middle of the night last week will fade and I'll come back to where I was before, only to hurt you once again and this time more deeply.

Response to fear #4: I don't see this happening. I'm just so open to doing this with you. I've had these little moments since then where the fear comes back and tries to get me to run away. It's trying to be more subtle, too, and dig deeper. I had a dream the other night where I woke up with that familiar "she's not the one" feeling (I'm sorry if it hurts or angers you to hear that I have had that feeling so much I call it familiar, but I think you knew that already and I can be honest about it now because I recognize it for what it is—actually, I think that deserves more of an explanation so you can understand; I'll provide one in another writing) and it almost got me to go along with it. It really is similar to alcoholism. My best thinking got me to the bottom of the bottle and the bottom of the bag of coke and the bottom of my darkness. The moments of clarity *are* fleeting. But they also transform you. And you have to honor them by doing the work in between when they come.

Fear #5: That I show you this and you hate it. It makes you want to leave even more. I guess that anything I do will make you want to leave even more.
