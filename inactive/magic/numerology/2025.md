Year
	sociability. others' perspectives. broadening world view. networking. other people fill in the missing pieces. while remaining true to self, personal integrity. guard against laxity, passivity. with so much association, choose supportive associates. emotional rollercoaster--enthusiasm, self-doubt. mentorship, outside counsel-->financial growth.
	finetune image: house, car, clothes, hair, with others' feedback. can be one of your greatest assets
January
	Introspection, not a jump into sociability.
	study, writing, reading, reflection
	time alone in nature
	dress well for weather, eat well, get good sleep
	imagination, visualization, daydreaming -- careful, they may manifest!
February
	Time to get busy, professionally. move forward assertively for quick, satisfying progress
	stable, objective, logical. clear-minded insights. home and family responsibilities. natural good judgment is attuned
March
	Regrouping, rebuilding, relaxing, resting
	tug of war: generous/inspirational vs retreating/cocoon
	stay viable while cocooning
April
	Promising, productive action. Implement new beginnings. Avoid lethargy.
	entreat positive-minded helpers
	guard against your own stubbornness/excessive candidness/cutting remarks
May
	Social whirlwind. give and take with charm and abandon. meeting new people, staying in touch with old. releasing inspiration into the world. nurture your affairs. guard against an avalanche of opinions and emotional hurt/pitfals
June
	Lots of creative enthusiasm, not necessarily channeled into new projects/beginnings. lots of conversation. don't try to get the last word. temper spontaneity in 2nd half of month
July
	Buckle down. work hard. practical, sensible, details. relationships: tend to problems and trials. set deeper roots. watch temper/words. stay close to home. careful diligence. health and safety.
August
	Nonstop action, flurries of excitement--don't careen out of control! optimism, passion, motivations, charisma, magnetism. yourself in top form. guard against over-indulgence
September
	expenditure of money and time. take care of the details as soon as they arise, including in relationships. take care of your home base / personal reserves. be scrupulous in finance. hold off on major decisions
October
	Thin skinned, sensitive, doubtful. solitude, introspection. ready, study, write. listen to dreams/daydreams. reassess and quietly nurture relationships.
November
	Tie up loose ends. do an audit and attend to the pending. tend to family, home, health, finances
December
	calm, restful. savor. reflect: what do you want to keep and what to let go? who to keep and who to let go? concretely plan for the year ahead. may experience self-doubt; don't mind that
