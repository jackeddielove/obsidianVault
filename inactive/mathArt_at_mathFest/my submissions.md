
ARTIST STATEMENT
I am a mathematician, artist, and educator living in San Francisco, CA. I have held faculty positions in academia but am currently finding my own way through the intersecting worlds of math, art, design, computation, and education. I visit K-12 classrooms to deliver mathematical demonstrations, volunteer as a math instructor at San Quentin Rehabilitation Center, and explore novel ways to use computers and technology for aesthetic and educational purposes.

There is only love
This work demonstrates the group of symmetries of a cube by depicting its 24 elements and how they are connected by a choice of two generators. Juxtaposed with the mathematical rigor is a philosophical musing.

We are already home
This piece demonstrates the symmetry group S_4 by depicting its 24 elements and the connections among them with respect to a certain generating set. Juxtaposed with the mathematical rigor is a philosophical musing.
