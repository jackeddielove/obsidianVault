1. **Your training**
	Describe your parents' romantic relationship as you were growing up
	- How did your parents deal with conflict?
		- I didn't see much conflict. I do remember one time--I was probably in elementary school--they were arguing and I said "Stop fighting", or something like that... in an angry, frustrated way, I said it.
	- Were your parents affectionate with each other?
		- I heard them having sex once. After I had moved out, I walked in on them dancing around the living room, naked. I don't think I saw them kiss hello or goodbye regularly. I do, however, seem to remember being grossed out by their shows of affection from time to time.
	- Did they verbally express their love to one another often?
		- I don't think so. We didn't really say 'I love you' as a family.
	- What qualities of your parents' relationship did you admire?
		- They were pretty independent. Each of them had things they were passionate about that they would do on their own, including leaving home for extended periods on their own. They also liked doing things together, watching movies, for example, or going out to dinner, or going to social events.
	- What qualities of your parents' relationship do you wish were different?
		- I can't really think of anything. They seem to have a nice relationship.
	- What messages did your parents give you about relationships/sexuality?
		- I saw that relationships work. That they last. That the can be intimate and long-lasting. They didn't say any of this to me explicitly. They never really talked to me about sex and relationships. In fact, they seemed to avoid it.

	Have you witnessed many successful relationships between men and women?
		I have seen a lot of them in my family. Lots of lifelong partnerships. Some of them seem strained at times. I see arguing. But they also seem loving. I guess they are successful if they last and the partners care about each other. If there doesn't seem to be any question about the relationship.
