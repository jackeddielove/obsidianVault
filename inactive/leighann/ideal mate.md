1. Define the qualities of your ideal romantic relationship
	1. single, unattached to anyone from their past
		Whether or not we have connections to past romantic partners, we are clear about what we mean to each other and the roles that we play in each other's lives. These roles may shift and change, and we are open to meeting those changes in partnership. The way that we engage in partnership may even mean separation. All of this is done in the spirit of love. 
	2. heterosexual/homosexual
		Whether or not we choose to define our sexual preferences, we freely question together and explore together what sexuality means to us. Sometimes, that may mean clear definitions. Other times, the lines may blur.
	3. spiritual affiliation
		We both understand our primary purpose is to grow spiritually and to turn over our will to something greater than ourselves
	4. good looking
		We both enjoy looking good, being stylish, expressing ourselves, and taking care of ourselves. Our good looks come more from our inner glow than anything else.
	5. healthy, nonsmoker, nondrinker, non-drugtaker
		Neither of us smokes or drinks or takes any mind altering substances. We both understand them as unnecessary distractions from the good orderly direction we all have access to all the time.
	6. romantic
		We show each other we care about one another. We surprise each other in little ways from day to day, year to year.
	7. friends and family
		We have healthy relationships with our families, and with each other's families. These relationships can be anything from close to distant, but the point is that we are at peace with them. We get along with each other's friends, and our friends are happy that we found each other.
	8. pets
		I have nothing to say about this one
	9. political affiliation
		I have nothing to say about this one
	10. great lovers/great chemistry/affectionate
		We are incredibly drawn to each other physically. The way we smell and taste to each other turns us on. We are very physically affectionate and have an active sexual life.
	11. financial life
		We each have our own finances well-sorted. We are comfortable and free from financial insecurity.
	12. extrovert/introvert
		She is extroverted and is very understanding that I am not. She doesn't need me to go with her to anything. It is simply not an issue. But I find it hot that she is out there being fun and outgoing and making people laugh and turning people on.
	13. faithful/honest/integrous
		We both have an understanding that dishonesty undermines our characters and our relationship. We have practices (the 10th step, for example) for addressing dishonesties--even little ones.
	14. children
		I don't want children and neither do they. They also don't have any children.
	15. work ethic
		We both have busy professional lives grounded in discipline. We understand that hard work is not the most important thing in the world, but it is indispensable.
	16. good communicator
		Communication is something we are always working on. We enjoy working on it. We want to know what we really think and where those thoughts come from and how to express them. We find that good communication is a practice for finding out who we are.
	17. nurturing
		We are both quite independent and don't need a lot of nurturing, though we don't hesitate to take care of one another when we need it.
	18. patient and forgiving, considerate of each other
		Each of us understands that when we forgo patience, forgiveness, and consideration, it is we (the forgoer) that suffers first, and then the relationship does. We also hold each other accountable. If one of us makes a mistake, we let them know, we take responsibility, we learn from it, and move forward.
	19. intellectual
		We are both very bright and have a natural curiosity about the world. Each of thinks the other one is smarter, and we joke about that.
	20. mentally balanced
		We both seek emotional balance. As soon as we notice imbalance in the form of thoughts, words, or actions, we recognize it and give it up. We recognize that it is not serving us, that it is sapping our energy, and is contributing to the chaos in the world.
	21. positive/joyful
		We enjoy laughing and being silly together. We know that it is appropriate to be sad sometimes, angry sometimes, disappointed sometimes, but there is a joy and a positivity always present.
	22. neat/tidy or messy
		We find joy in curating our spaces.
	23. funny and lighthearted
		We make each other laugh. We make each other smile. We can ease the tension of a moment with a simple look or phrase.
	24. common interests
		We actually have very different interests, but they are bound by a commonality: beauty and depth and joy.
	25. committed/works on relationship
		We recognize that this relationship is an opportunity to continue to grow spiritually, and that it will take commitment and discipline to do it.
	26. unconditional love
		We know that we are not each other's higher power, each other's supreme love. Instead, we offer each other the kind of love that allows each of us to grow closer to that spiritual love.
