# Framework
## students
read short intro and complete preview activity before class
spend time in class working with peers
work in your activities workbook (can we provide this? print out the relevant pages every week?)
read the summary after each section. also, try to write your own.
anonymous webwork exercises--infinite attempts, no penalties
[this short video on the HTML version that includes a WeBWorK demonstration](http://gvsu.edu/s/14i)
If your institution uses WeBWorK, you may also need to log in to a server as directed by your instructor to complete assigned WeBWorK sets as part of your course grade. The WeBWorK exercises included in this text are ungraded and not connected to any individual account. (do we use webwork? can I assign and have these assignments graded?)
3-4 additional challenge exercises

## instructors
1 section ~ 2 hours of class meeting time (perfect)
typical instructional sequence:
- 10 minutes: students complete preview activity before class. debrief among peers followed by all class discussion
- 10 minutes: lecture/discussion to build on preview activity and set stage for next activity
- 20 minutes: students work together on activity 1
- 15 minutes: discussion/lecture to close activity 1 and prepare for activity 2
- 20 minutes: students work together on activity 2
- 15 minutes: discussion/lecture to close activity 2 and prepare for exercises
