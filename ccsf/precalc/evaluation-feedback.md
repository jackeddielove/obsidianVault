**4. Understanding AB 1705 and the Purpose of Math 90S**

Since you are new to the department, we encourage you to learn more about AB 1705 and how it has affected our students. Since AB 1705 was implemented, students no longer have the option to take pre-algebra and intermediate algebra courses. As a result, many students are entering our classes without a strong foundation in algebra. Some still struggle with basic skills, such as solving a simple linear equation.

Math 90S is intended to provide extra support for these students. The goal is to give them more time to work on problems they struggled with during the regular class. For this reason, Math 90S time should focus on reviewing course material and strengthening students' basic skills, rather than introducing new or more advanced topics, such as the logistic function.

Our goal is to make sure the material aligns with the course outlines and that students receive the step-by-step instruction and support they need to succeed.
