(c) TB Clearance:  Risk assessment, skin or blood test result or x-ray are accepted, and must be dated within the last 6 months with a valid Medical Providers signature. If you currently work at another educational institution and you have a TB clearance on file with that institution, a copy of that TB clearance can be submitted as long as it meets our requirements.
	(note from Justin: You may want to reach out to the previous provider though. They might be able to provide an updated assessment based on the last time you contacted them. It must include your name, the provider's name and contact information, the results, and the date of assessment/skin test/blood test.)

- _Please bring your eligible TB document to your onboarding appointment_
