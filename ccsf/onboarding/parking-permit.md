If you are a **new employee** and have **completed your onboarding**, please visit the Public Safety Department, as part of your final step for onboarding, for a temporary pass and registration for an Employee Parking Pass. After this is completed, you will receive an email with instructions on how to obtain your employee hangtag parking permit. The Public Safety Department is in the [Judson Village in Bungalow 800](https://www.ccsf.edu/about/our-locations/map/bungalow-800 "Bungalow 800"). For more information, please call 415-239-3200.
