# unit rates
1. A 12-ounce bottle of shampoo costs $3.60. What is the unit price per ounce?
	1. 30 cents per ounce
2. A cyclist rides 36 miles in 3 hours at a constant speed. What is the cyclist's unit rate in miles per hour?
3. A robot can assemble 64 toy cars in 4 minutes. What is the robot's unit work rate in cars per minute?
4. At the grocery store, a 6-pack of yogurt costs $7.00. How much does a single yogurt cost?
5. A train travels 210 miles in 3 hours. Assuming it travels at a constant speed, how fast is it traveling in miles per hour?
# proportional relationships
A proportional relationship between two variables $x, y$ is one in which $y=kx$ for some constant $k$.
1. Which equation represents a proportional relationship between $x$ and $y$?
	1. $y=2x+3$
	2. $y=\frac{2}{3}x$
	3. $y=x^2$
	4. $y=\frac{4}{x}$
2. A table shows that 3 pounds of apples cost $\$6.00$, and 5 pounds cost $\$10.00$. What is the constant of proportionality ($k$) in dollars per pound?
3. What are the two requirements for a graph to show a proportional relationship?
4. The number of cups of flour ($y$) is proportional to the number of batches of cookies ($x$). If 3 batches require 6 cups of flour, how many cups are needed for 7 batches? 

# scale drawings
1. On a map, the scale is 1 inch = 5 miles. If the distance between two cities on the map is 4.5 inches, what is the actual distance between them?
2. An architect is drawing a floor plan for a room that is 16 feet long. If the scale of the drawing is 0.5 inches = 2 feet, how long should the room be in the drawing?
3. A rectangular playground has an actual area of 400 square meters. A blueprint uses a scale where 1 cm = 4 meters. What is the area of the playground on the blueprint? 
4. A model airplane has a wingspan of 9 inches. The actual airplane has a wingspan of 27 feet. What is the scale of the model in inches to feet?
5. If a triangle $T_1$ has an area of 10 square units, and triangle $T_2$ is a scaled copy of $T_1$ with a scale factor of 3, what is the area of triangle $T_2$?
6. If polygon $P_1$ has an area of 1 square unit and polygon $P_2$ is a scaled copy of $P_1$ with an area of 25 square units, what is the scale factor of $P_2$ with respect to $P_1$?

# pythagorean challenges
1. A right triangle has legs with lengths of 6 cm and 8 cm. What is the length of the hypotenuse?
2. The hypotenuse of a right triangle is 13 inches long. One of the legs is 5 inches long. What is the length of the other leg?
3. A 15-foot ladder is leaning against a wall. The bottom of the ladder is placed 9 feet away from the base of the wall. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?
4. How far is the point $(5, 12)$ for the origin, $(0, 0)$, in the coordinate plane?
5. What is the distance between the points (1, 2) and (4, 6) on a coordinate plane?
6. How far is the point $(10, 4, 7)$ from the origin $(0, 0, 0)$, in $\mathbb{R}^3$?
7. How far is the point $(10, 4, 7)$ from the point $(3, 17, 29)$, in $\mathbb{R}^3$?
